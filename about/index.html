About Textable

Your comprehensive task management solution

Welcome to Textable

Textable is a powerful task management platform that seamlessly integrates with Google Sheets to help you organize, track, and manage your tasks efficiently. Whether you're working solo or as part of a team, Textable provides the tools you need to stay productive and on top of your workload.

Key Features

🔒 Secure Login

Protected access with encrypted authentication ensures your data stays safe and secure.

📋 Task Organization

Organize tasks by categories and sheets for better project and workflow management.

⚡ Real-time Sync

Seamless integration with Google Sheets keeps your data synchronized across platforms.

💳 Credit System

Flexible credit-based usage model gives you control over your spending and resource allocation.

👨‍💼 Admin Controls

Manage users and credits with ease through our intuitive admin interface.

📱 Mobile Friendly

Access your tasks on any device with our responsive design that works on mobile and desktop.

Getting Started

Getting started with Textable is easy:

  1. Create an Account: Sign up with your credentials to get instant access to your personal dashboard.
  2. Set Up Categories: Organize your tasks by creating categories that match your workflow.
  3. Add Tasks: Start adding tasks to your categories and assign notes for better context.
  4. Manage Credits: Monitor your credit balance and top up when needed to continue using premium features.
